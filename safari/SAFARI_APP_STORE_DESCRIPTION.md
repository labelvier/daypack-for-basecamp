## What's New in This Version

Initial release with smart filtering for Basecamp card tables.

## Description

Daypack adds smart filtering options directly to your Basecamp card tables (kanban boards), helping you stay focused and productive.

### Key Features

• Filter by Assignee - Instantly view only the cards assigned to specific team members
• Filter by Author - See who created which cards and track contributions
• Multi-Filter Support - Combine filters to get exactly the view you need
• One-Click Clearing - Reset filters instantly to see the full board again

### Why Daypack?

Save Time: Stop scrolling through endless cards looking for specific tasks. Find what you need in seconds.

Stay Focused: When you're working on your tasks, see only what's relevant to you. Eliminate distractions and boost productivity.

Better Team Collaboration: Quickly check team members' workloads and track who's responsible for what.

Seamless Integration: Works directly within Basecamp's interface. No need to learn new tools or leave your workflow.

### Privacy & Security

Daypack runs entirely in your browser. We don't collect, store, or transmit any of your Basecamp data. Your project information stays private and secure.

### Requirements

• macOS 10.14 or later
• Safari 14 or later
• Active Basecamp 3 account

Note: This extension only works on Basecamp 3 card tables (kanban boards).
