# Firefox Add-ons (AMO) Store Description

**Name:** Backpack by Label Vier

**Summary (max 250 chars):**
All your Basecamp tools, always at hand in your backpack. Filter kanban boards by assignee, author, and tags. Add, edit, and organize tags directly on cards. Stay organized effortlessly.

**Description:**
Backpack by Label Vier brings powerful filtering and organization tools to your Basecamp 4 card tables (kanban boards).

**Features:**

🔍 **Smart Filtering**
- Filter cards by assignee, author, or tag
- Multiple filter options that work together
- Filters persist across page navigation
- Visual column counts show filtered results

🏷️ **Tag Management**
- Add and remove tags directly on cards
- Tags display as visual pills on cards and detail pages
- Click any tag to filter instantly
- Color-coded tags for easy recognition

✨ **Seamless Integration**
- Matches Basecamp's native design
- Works with Basecamp's Turbo navigation
- No data collection or tracking
- Lightweight and fast

**Perfect for teams that need:**
- Better organization of large card tables
- Quick filtering by team member or tag
- Visual tag system for project management
- Enhanced productivity in Basecamp

**Privacy:**
This extension runs entirely in your browser. We don't collect, store, or transmit any of your Basecamp data.

**Supported Basecamp version:**
Basecamp 4 (3.basecamp.com)
