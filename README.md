# All your Basecamp tools, always at hand in your daypack. Filter kanban boards, manage tasks, and stay organized.

Tags and filtering in Basecamp!

Daypack by Label Vier supercharges your Basecamp workflow by adding powerful filtering capabilities to your kanban boards. Stay focused, organised, and in control of your projects.

## 🎯 What It Does

Daypack adds smart filtering options directly to your Basecamp card tables (kanban boards), allowing you to:

- Filter by Assignee: Instantly view only the cards assigned to specific team members
- Filter by Author: See who created which cards and track contributions
- Multi-Filter Support: Combine filters to get exactly the view you need
- Share filters: Share a filtered view with your fellow basecampers by url
